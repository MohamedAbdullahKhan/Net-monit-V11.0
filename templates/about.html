Net-monit V11.0

Net-monit is a production-grade, self-hosted network monitoring platform. Monitor ping latency, packet loss, CPU, memory, disk, and bandwidth across your infrastructure; monitor web application uptime and SSL expiry; run WAN speed tests; discover devices with a network scanner; and receive email alerts with configurable thresholds, multi-level escalation routing, and a full Acknowledge/Resolve alert workflow.
